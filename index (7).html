GoldPlot Privacy Policy & Offline Data Architecture

Applies to the GoldPlot mobile application developed by Firefly Labz.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Backend Servers

GoldPlot is designed as a strict offline-first utility application. We do not maintain any external cloud servers, remote databases, or user account systems. Firefly Labz does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, survey coordinates, property valuations, or device identifiers.

2. No Login or Account Registration

Users are never required to create an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as name, email address, phone number, or billing details) to access GoldPlot's surveying, mapping, or valuation tools.

3. On-Device Local Storage Only

Any polygons, parcel notes, or custom unit configurations you choose to save are stored exclusively inside your device's local sandboxed storage (via local Room database). Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage permanently erases all local records, as no cloud backup exists on our servers.

4. Device Permissions (GPS / Location)

GoldPlot requests access to your device's Fine Location (GPS) solely to display your live position on the map canvas and record boundary vertices during active field surveying. Location coordinates are processed locally in real time and are never transmitted to Firefly Labz.

5. Optional Online Map & Proximity Queries

While all calculators, geometry solvers, and GPS polygon tools work 100% offline, if you choose to load online vector/satellite map tiles (MapLibre) or run an optional Proximity Audit (OpenStreetMap / Overpass API) while connected to the internet, standard map tile/query requests are sent directly from your device to those public geospatial tile providers without any personal user profile attached.

Professional & Legal Disclaimer

Important notice regarding field measurements, cadastral conversions, and financial estimates.

1. Not a Substitute for Certified Legal Land Surveys

GoldPlot is an informational and preliminary field estimation tool. Commercial smartphone GPS receivers have inherent atmospheric and hardware tolerances (typically ±1 to ±5 meters). Measurements generated by GoldPlot do not constitute a legally binding cadastral or revenue survey. For official property registration, boundary disputes, or legal deeds, always engage a government-licensed land surveyor using calibrated Total Station or RTK-GNSS equipment.

2. Regional Unit Variations

Traditional land measurement units (such as Bigha, Biswa, Guntha, Kanal, Marla, and Cent) vary across states, districts, and historical jurisdictions. While GoldPlot provides pre-calibrated regional presets, users are responsible for verifying the exact local conversion factor applicable to their specific revenue circle.

3. Property Pricing & Tax Estimations

Calculations generated by the Property Pricing & Acquisition Estimator (including base valuation, stamp duty, registration charges, and administrative fees) are indicative approximations based on user-entered percentages and rates. Actual statutory duties and government circle rates are subject to local municipal and state tax regulations.

4. Third-Party Geospatial Data (Overpass / OSM)

Proximity audit results (nearby highways, powerlines, water bodies, railways) rely on crowd-sourced OpenStreetMap data via the Overpass API. Firefly Labz makes no warranties regarding the completeness or real-time accuracy of third-party map layers.
